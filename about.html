Our Team

Ivan Kozlov

Ivan Kozlov joined Quilxotic after the master class, where his portraits intertwining classics with mental depth conquered students. His approach is a bridge between traditions and bold self -expression.

Maria Smirnova

Maria Smirnova joined Quilxotic when her groundbreaking NFT collection, merging folklore with cyberpunk aesthetics, revolutionized our digital curriculum. Her vision redefines storytelling through pixels.

Alexei Petrov

Alexei Petrov joined Quilxotic after his viral "Geometric Phoenix" mural sparked a street art movement. Now he teaches how to turn concrete jungles into visionary canvases

The Evolution of Quilxotic

Founding (2018)

Quilxotic began as a Moscow loft studio where 5 artists taught painting basics through intimate workshops.

Digital Leap (2020)

The pandemic inspired our first online sketching course, reaching 1,000+ students globally.

Global Expansion (2022)

We launched multilingual programs and recruited masters like Kozlov and Smirnova.

Innovation Era (2024)

Now featuring AI feedback assistants and a VR student gallery.
